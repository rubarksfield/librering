# Code of conduct

LibreRing is built around care: for users, their private data, and each other.

## Our standards

We welcome curiosity, precise disagreement, patient explanation, and work that
makes the project safer or easier to understand. We do not tolerate harassment,
discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, deliberate exposure of private
data, or sustained disruption of the community.

## Health and privacy discussions

Do not pressure anyone to share health records, location, device identifiers,
credentials, or raw captures. Remove accidental sensitive data from quotations
and screenshots before posting. Technical disagreement must stay focused on
evidence and behaviour, not a person's health or identity.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose behaviour is harmful. To report conduct privately, contact
the maintainer through the GitHub profile at
[`@rubarksfield`](https://github.com/rubarksfield); do not place sensitive
details in a public issue.
