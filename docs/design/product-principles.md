# Product principles

1. **Conclusion before numbers.** Say what changed and why before showing a wall
   of metrics.
2. **Personal normal before judgement.** Compare robust personal history while
   retaining evidence-based guardrails and context.
3. **Provenance is content.** Measured, firmware-estimated, app-derived, imported,
   user-entered, unsupported, and unknown must not look identical.
4. **Confidence is separate.** Missing or uncertain data changes confidence or
   produces no result; it is not bad physiology.
5. **Never fabricate hardware.** No temperature, respiration, R–R, raw motion,
   swim detection, laps, calories-as-truth, or medical oximetry on R12.
6. **Local first.** Core use has no account, cloud, tracking, analytics, or AI.
7. **Progressive disclosure.** Glance answers, Explain contributors, Inspect raw
   evidence and calculation details.
8. **Rest is legitimate.** Illness, recovery days, pregnancy context, disability,
   and unseen activities must not be moralised as failure.
9. **One action, not a lecture.** Recommendations are conservative, optional, and
   never claim how someone feels.
10. **Failure is visible and recoverable.** Show last known good state, preserve
    data, bound retries, and explain what the app cannot repair.
11. **Open by design.** Formula, confidence, model version, export, and protocol
    evidence are inspectable.
12. **Original calm.** Avoid competitor rings, colour bands, layouts, score names,
    and visual metaphors as the organising identity.

## Copy rules

Use “estimate,” “appears,” “available signals,” and “compared with your recent
pattern.” Avoid “optimal body,” “bad recovery,” “detected illness,” “biological
age,” and “your body says.” Put limitations next to the affected result, not in a
legal page alone.
