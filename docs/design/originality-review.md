reference's generic type and card grammar: neutral grotesk type, oversized
tabular numerals, a light-grey canvas, flat modular cards, coral heat cells, and
compact charts. Its screen content, measurement boundaries, confidence and
provenance labels, navigation, missing-data grammar, pairing sequence, swim
limitation card, and Cycle Context consent flow remain LibreRing-specific. All
rendered artwork is authored in HTML/CSS/SVG inside the prototype.

The approved Open Design artifact preserves those boundaries with an original
SVG ring illustration, LibreRing-authored charts and copy, a separate lead
heatmap and four-card metrics view, and an honest unsupported-reading state. It
loads no external reference asset, font, image, script, or stylesheet.
