Garmin Connect, Fitbit, and Zepp were included as ecosystem-level comparator
patterns. Their broad-app conclusions should be refreshed through direct current
build playtests before any feature-level competitive claim is published.

The 2024 Oura redesign sources were refreshed on 2026-08-24. The official article
and complete English subtitles were read directly. Public YouTube storyboards
were sampled across the full 96-second film to inspect composition and feature
sequence. The requested watch-video Gemini analysis was attempted twice (the
initial call plus its one permitted retry) but both returned HTTP 503, so no
Gemini-generated visual observations are treated as evidence.

The two Dribbble concepts were reviewed on 2026-08-24 from their rendered shot
media and creator descriptions. After the user selected the smart-ring concept
as the rigid visual reference—including its fonts and card style—the rendered
prototype was rebuilt around its broad visual grammar: one neutral grotesk,
assertive tabular numerals, flat grey modular cards, compact labels, a two-by-two
metric grid, and coral data marks. The mood-tracker concept no longer informs the
rendered build. Concept-screen physiological claims, branding, exact geometry,
phone/ring imagery, icons, and media were not transferred. The Dribbble shot is a
visual reference, not usability, scientific-validity, accessibility, or
production evidence.
