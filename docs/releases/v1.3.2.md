# LibreRing 1.3.2 — Fresh readings, clearer feedback

Your ring. Your data. Kept close.

This release makes everyday use more predictable: pull to refresh the readings
already on your phone, use **Sync** to fetch history from your ring, and get
restrained tactile feedback when an action or save succeeds.

## What's new

- **Pull to refresh saved readings** on Today, Vitals, Trends, Activity, Sleep,
  Heart, Oxygen, firmware signal details and the day timeline—even on short or
  empty pages. The gesture does not start Bluetooth sync.
- **Newly saved readings appear immediately.** Fixed a cached-clock issue that
  could hide recent samples until the next one-minute tick. A successful sync
  updates the view automatically, including a previously visited Vitals tab.
- **More dependable chart inspection.** Historical dates stay selected, changed
  data clears stale inspected samples, and Trends keeps the same calendar day
  selected across midnight.
- **Purposeful haptics** for navigation, selection, chart boundaries, refresh,
  sync and save/delete results. Disabled controls and background updates stay
  quiet; chart feedback is deduplicated and throttled.
- **Clearer live-pulse help.** The Heart page explains saved versus live readings.
  Live pulse and oxygen remain disabled until usable readings and reliable
  stop/cancel behavior are demonstrated on the physical ring.

The current experience also includes the refinements from 1.3.1: visible sync
stages and waiting states, plain-language HRV/stress/energy explanations,
context-based daily guidance, explicit last-reported battery information, and
a consistent green sleep palette with horizontal or hold-and-slide inspection.

## Scope and installation

**This is a development-preview source release, not an App Store, TestFlight or
Play Store distribution.** GitHub's source archives contain the project;
attached media is a showcase, not an installer. Personal signing credentials,
the development-signed iOS app, and health exports are not distributed.

Build from source using the
[quick start](https://github.com/rubarksfield/librering/tree/v1.3.2#quick-start).
iPhone Home Screen launch requires a profile or release build and your own
valid signing setup. Demo mode uses fictional data and requires no ring.

Production connectivity is scoped to the documented COLMI R12 firmware path.
Firmware HRV/stress fields remain unverified indexes; the ring energy field is
not presented as confirmed active calories. No clinical accuracy, recovery
score, live measurement, GPS or compatibility with other ring brands is claimed.
Physical refresh/haptic feel and further real-ring sync acceptance remain to be
checked. LibreRing is not a medical device.

Contributions are welcome in Flutter UX, accessibility, Android device testing,
privacy and carefully consented protocol validation. Start with the
[contribution guide](https://github.com/rubarksfield/librering/blob/v1.3.2/CONTRIBUTING.md)
and never post personal health records or device identifiers in public issues.
